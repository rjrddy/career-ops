# Cover Letter — Forage, Software Engineer

---

I am writing to apply for the Software Engineer position at Forage.

Payments infrastructure is usually invisible until it fails someone. What draws me to Forage is that you have made that invisibility a mission: 42 million people on SNAP EBT deserve seamless access to groceries, and right now most of the digital commerce ecosystem just does not work for them. Building the stack that fixes that is exactly the kind of problem I want to spend my time on. Not because it is technically interesting (it is), but because the people on the other side of a successful checkout actually feel it.

I want to work at a company where I can own things end to end, not just pick up tickets. At Doxy.me, I was involved from architecture conversations through production releases, and that full-cycle responsibility made me a much better engineer than any environment where I was handed a scoped spec and told to implement it. I want more of that at Forage. I want to sit in product reviews, push back on requirements when the implementation has a smarter path, ship something, watch real users interact with it, and go fix what breaks. That ownership is what I am looking for in a next role, and it is clearly how Forage operates.

My background maps well to what you are building. I have shipped production Python backend services and React frontends at Doxy.me, deployed and operated AWS infrastructure (SageMaker, Fargate), and built CI/CD pipelines across cloud environments. At L3Harris I work on microservices with strict reliability constraints, which translates directly to payment systems where correctness is non-negotiable. And at HEXstream I built ETL pipelines integrating 25+ enterprise data sources, so the data layer that sits behind financial infrastructure is familiar ground.

More than the stack, I want to be in a room where people care about what they are building. Forage's culture around humility and ownership is genuinely what I am looking for. I am early in my career and I want to grow fast, but that requires being somewhere where the work matters and the team pushes each other. From everything I have read about how Forage operates, that is the environment you have built.
