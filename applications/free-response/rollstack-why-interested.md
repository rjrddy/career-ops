# Free Response: "Why are you interested in working at Rollstack?"

**Company:** Rollstack  
**Role:** SWE Fullstack (YC)  
**Date:** July 2026  

---

## Answer

The problem Rollstack solves is one I've watched cause real friction — analysts and engineers manually rebuilding the same decks every reporting cycle because their data and their documents live in separate worlds. Rollstack closes that gap in a way that actually scales.

What draws me specifically is that the technical work sits at the intersection I find most interesting: data pipelines feeding a real-time frontend. That's exactly what I built at HEXstream and again at Doxy.me — systems where the hard part isn't any one layer, it's making them talk reliably. The fact that SoFi and Zillow are already depending on it tells me the core product works. I want to be part of what comes next.
