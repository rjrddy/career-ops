## Answer

At Doxy.me, I built a multimodal AI diagnostic tool using PyTorch, Llama, and WebRTC that analyzed visual cues, vocal tone, and speech patterns from live telehealth sessions in real time, deploying the inference pipeline on SageMaker and Fargate to surface mental health condition insights directly to the treating physician. The tool used reinforcement learning to continuously improve diagnostic accuracy and was integrated into the clinical workflow, giving doctors AI-generated signals during patient sessions that previously required specialist referrals to obtain.
