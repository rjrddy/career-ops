# Free Response: "What are you looking for in your next role? What would you like to avoid?"

**Used for:** General / Confido  
**Date:** July 2026  

---

## Answer

I'm looking for a startup where ownership is real, not a talking point. I want to be the person who ships a feature on Monday and hears about it from a customer on Wednesday — not someone waiting six weeks for a design review to clear four teams.

I want to work with the tools that are actually moving the industry right now: LLMs, agent frameworks, modern data infrastructure. Not because they're trendy, but because that's where the interesting problems are.

What I want to avoid: legacy codebases maintained by process rather than judgment, environments where growth means waiting your turn, and anywhere that treats engineering as a cost center. I've had a taste of fast, high-stakes, high-ownership work — that's the standard I'm measuring against.
