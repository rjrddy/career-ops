## Salary Context

- Austin salaries: $100K–$170K for SWE/ML at growth/public companies
- Cost of living: ~25–30% below NYC (no state income tax in Texas)
- Effective purchasing power: $130K Austin ≈ $175K+ NYC
- No state income tax = meaningful take-home advantage vs. NYC

## Notes

- Austin is a major tech hub (Tesla, Apple, Meta, Google all have large offices)
- Most roles here are US-only — verify visa sponsorship for each company
- Built In Austin is the primary local job board to check regularly
